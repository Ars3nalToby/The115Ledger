What the rules say

Premier League Rule W.51 of the Handbook gives an independent commission a broad range of sanctions if breaches are proven: a reprimand, an unlimited fine, suspension, a points deduction (which can be applied retrospectively to a completed season), expulsion from the league, or "such other order as it thinks fit".

This wording is sourced to journalists' citations of the rule (The Athletic, BBC's Dan Roan) and a legal-industry analysis of the Premier League's disciplinary process, not a direct quotation from the current Handbook — see Sources & Methodology for the citations and this caveat.

How other clubs compare

Everton, Nottingham Forest and Leicester City have all been sanctioned under the Premier League's Profitability and Sustainability Rules (PSR) in recent seasons. None of these cases involves the same charges as Manchester City's — City's case includes UEFA financial rules and non-cooperation charges that the others do not — but they show the range of sanctions the Premier League has actually applied.
