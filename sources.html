Every source used on this site, grouped by page; how the status labels work; and how corrections are handled.

How status labels work

Every claim on this site about the case itself carries one of these labels, used precisely:

CHARGED / ALLEGED
Before the independent commission's decision.
FOUND (reported)
The verdict as reported by journalists (for example, The Athletic), before the commission's written decision is published.
FOUND (published decision)
Only once the commission's written reasons are public — linked to the document itself.
SANCTION PENDING
A finding exists but no sanction has been announced yet.
UNDER APPEAL
A sanction has been announced and is being appealed by a party to the case.
FINAL
The matter is resolved and not subject to further appeal.
