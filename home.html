Where the case stands today

In February 2023 the Premier League charged Manchester City with 115 alleged breaches of its financial rules, covering the 2009-10 to 2017-18 seasons plus an alleged failure to cooperate with the investigation from December 2018 onward. An independent commission heard the case in London between September and December 2024. On 29 September 2026 the Premier League published a redacted version of the commission's Core Decision: City were found in breach on every charge bar one, covering all the financial-rules charges and three of four cooperation categories, including what the commission called a disguised funding scheme of “sham” sponsorship arrangements part-funded by owner ADUG. The status is FOUND (published decision); the appendices are still to be published. On 1 October City lodged an appeal, which an independent three-member Appeal Board will hear, with a hearing expected within 12 weeks; the club says the opinion is unsafe and that it is innocent. The sanction is still to be decided at a separate hearing. See the full timeline and every source behind this summary.
