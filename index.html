Where the case stands today

In February 2023 the Premier League charged Manchester City with 115 alleged breaches of its financial rules, covering the 2009-10 to 2017-18 seasons plus an alleged failure to cooperate with the investigation from December 2018 onward. An independent commission heard the case in London between September and December 2024. On 25 September 2026, David Ornstein of The Athletic reported that the commission had found City guilty on 114 of the 115 charges — a reported verdict, not yet the commission's own published written decision. Sanctions have not been announced and City are expected to appeal. See the full timeline and every source behind this summary.
