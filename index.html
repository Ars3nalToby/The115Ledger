What this site is

The 115 Ledger tracks Manchester City's spending, revenues and regulatory history from the 2008 Abu Dhabi takeover to the Premier League's case alleging 115 breaches of its financial rules — and its outcome. Every factual claim carries an outlet, a date and a link. Every major event carries the club's own response. Where sources disagree on a figure, this site shows the range and says so.

It is not affiliated with any club, league or governing body, and it is not a fan campaign — see the full methodology and status-label glossary for how every label and figure on this site is decided.
